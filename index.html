Conference Proceedings · Academy of Management

Unlocking Generative AI Adoption: Innovation Capabilities, Alliances, and Implementation Strategy

Diptikanta Panigrahi, Sumanta Singha, Vincent L. Barker

Analysis of 296 S&P 500 firms (2023–2024) reveals GenAI adoption generates positive abnormal returns, but returns diminish for firms with higher innovation capabilities unless complemented by strategic alliances or core-function implementation. Firms with stronger exploratory orientation achieve significantly higher returns when combining partnerships with core implementation.
