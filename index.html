Research Statement

I study how firms orchestrate transformative technologies — from generative AI to strategic alliances — to build sustainable competitive advantage. My work bridges quantitative rigor from a decade in digital analytics with strategic management theory, examining how top management cognition, innovation capabilities, and organizational learning shape value creation in an era of autonomous and generative systems.

Conference Proceedings · 2025

Unlocking Generative AI Adoption: Innovation Capabilities, Alliances, and Implementation Strategy

Diptikanta Panigrahi (corresponding), Sumanta Singha, Vincent L. Barker

Academy of Management Proceedings, 2025

Analysis of 296 S&P 500 firms (2023–2024) reveals that GenAI adoption generates positive abnormal returns, but returns diminish for firms with higher innovation capabilities unless complemented by strategic alliances or core-function implementation. Firms with stronger exploratory orientation achieve higher returns when combining partnerships with core function deployment.
