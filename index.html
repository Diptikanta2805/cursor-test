My dissertation program examines how resource orchestration, strategic choices, and top-management attention shape competitive advantage in the age of autonomous and generative technologies.

Academy of Management Proceedings · 2025

Unlocking Generative AI Adoption: Innovation Capabilities, Alliances, and Implementation Strategy

with Sumanta Singha & Vincent L. Barker

Hover to read abstract →

Analysis of 296 S&P 500 firms (2023–2024) reveals GenAI adoption generates positive abnormal returns — but returns diminish for highly innovative firms unless complemented by strategic alliances or core-function implementation. Firms with stronger exploratory innovation orientation achieve higher returns when combining partnerships with core-function GenAI deployment.
